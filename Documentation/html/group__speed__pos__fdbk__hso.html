Detailed Description

Speed & Position feedback HSO component of the Motor Control SDK.

This component provides the speed and the angular position of the rotor based on a sensorless approach.

By considering a perfect measurements of three phase currents and voltages with knowledge of stator resistance Rs and inductance Ls the back-emf can be estimated. The electrical angle is then provided by the High Sensitivity Observer after an estimation of the flux based on the back-emf.
