Rotor speed & position feedback

Prev: Current sensing and protection using embedded OpAmps and Comparators ↤| Table of Contents |↦ Next: Speed & position feedback sensorless algorithms

Introduction to the PMSM FOC drive shows that rotor position/speed measurement has a crucial role in PMSM field-oriented control. Hall sensors or encoders are broadly used in the control chain for that purpose. Sensorless algorithms for rotor position/speed feedback are considered very useful for various reasons: to lower the overall cost of the application, to enhance the reliability by redundancy, and so on. Refer to Speed & position feedback sensorless algorithms, Hall sensor feedback processing, and Encoder sensor feedback processing for further details.

The selection of speed/position feedback is performed with the ST Motor Control Workbench tool.
