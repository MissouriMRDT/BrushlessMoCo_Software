void MC_POLPULSE_run (MC_PolPulse_Handle_t *pHandle, const fixp30_t angle_pu)
 Executes PolPulse procedure. The PolPulse is initiated at each transition to close-loop speed or torque control. When the PolPulse is trigged, the PWM channels are activated here according to the pulses duration. The computed duties will be used throughout the pulse period. Once pulse sequence is completed the estimated angle is used to initialize the start angle for the observer (HSO).

 
void PulseCountDown (MC_PolPulse_Handle_t *pHandle)
 handles pulse generation. The PWM is generated during the countdown period and switched off automatically at end. It should be called during TIMx CC4 IRQ handler.
