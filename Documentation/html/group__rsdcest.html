Detailed Description

Rs DC estimation process.

RS DC estimator component of the Motor Control SDK.

The resistance estimator starts with an offset corrected inverter, running at 50% duty. Current \(i_d\) is ramped up to a preset value in about 250 ms to allow for smooth alignment in case the rotor is mis-aligned. During the ramp-up, filters are calculated at interrupt frequency to low-pass filter \(u_{\alpha\beta LP}\) and \(i_{\alpha\beta LP}\) After about 750 ms from the start of the ramp, the resistance is estimated by \( Rs = \frac {u_{\alpha\beta LP}}{i_{\alpha\beta LP}} \). Figure below shows the sequence for a mis-aligned motor that aligns after 300 ms after the start of the ramp. The ZeST injection is started half-way the ramp-down to ensure demodulator readiness at the transit to speed or torque control.

◆ RsDC_estimationState_t

enum RsDC_estimationState_t

Lists all possible Rs DC estimation states.

Enumerator
RSDC_EST_RESET 

No polarization offset have been provided to or measured by the Motor Control application since the application was started.

RSDC_EST_ONGOING 

The polarization offset measurement procedure is on-going. The Motor cannot be controlled yet.

RSDC_EST_COMPLETED 

The polarization offsets measurement procedure has completed or polarization offsets have been provided with MC_SetPolarizationOffsetsMotor1().
